## rnaseq_expression

- **Status**: complete — 8 public RNA-seq runs (Bd JEL423 3×, Bsal AMFP13 5×) aligned to the NCBI reference via STAR, then **per-gene counts recomputed with featureCounts** from gffread-converted GTFs. STAR's own `--quantMode GeneCounts` produced no gene counts (NCBI GFF3 exon lines carry no `gene_id` attribute) — fixed with `gffread -T` (GTF) + `featureCounts` (unstranded `-s 0`, the best-assigning mode) over the existing BAMs. `build_expression_evidence.py` maps each linkage candidate BFD protein through its RBH NCBI ortholog to a locus and folds `rna_is_expressed` / `gene_total_raw` / `n_rep_ge_min` / `rna_no_evidence` into `{dendrobatidis,salamandrivorans}_candidate_table.tsv`.
- **Purpose**: 4th independent (measured) boolean in the linkage tiering — presence/absence expression evidence for candidate biosynthetic genes (NOT condition-matched co-expression; these runs are not from the LC-MS growth condition).
